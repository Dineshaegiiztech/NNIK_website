Step 2: How to Pack Your Parcel for USA Shipping

Proper packaging is critical for international shipping. Poorly packed parcels can get damaged in transit and may be rejected by customs. Follow these best practices:

Packaging Guidelines for USA-Bound Parcels

  • Use double-wall corrugated cardboard boxes — single-wall boxes are not strong enough for international transit.
  • Wrap each item individually in bubble wrap or foam sheets. For fragile items, use at least 5cm of cushioning on all sides.
  • Seal all openings with strong H-tape. Use at least 3 layers of packing tape on all seams and edges.
  • Place a packing list inside the parcel with item descriptions and values — this helps with customs clearance.
  • Label clearly on the outside with sender and recipient names, complete addresses, and phone numbers.
  • Do not use newspaper as padding — it can damage printed materials and looks unprofessional to customs officers.
💡 Pro Tip from NRI Xpress:

Volumetric weight can be higher than actual weight for bulky but light items (e.g., clothing). Volumetric weight = (L × W × H in cm) ÷ 5000. You'll be charged whichever is higher. Pack compactly to save on shipping cost!

Step 3: Understanding US Customs Rules for India Parcels

Every parcel entering the United States must clear US Customs and Border Protection (CBP). Here's what you need to know:

Duty-Free Threshold for USA

The US has a de minimis threshold of $800 per shipment per day. This means if the total declared value of your parcel is $800 or less, no import duties are charged. Above this amount, standard import duties apply (typically 0–25% depending on the product category).

Restricted & Prohibited Items for USA Shipping

  • ❌ Prohibited: Narcotics, firearms, counterfeit goods, live animals, hazardous materials, certain agricultural products
  • ⚠️ Restricted: Medicines (require valid prescription), food items (must be commercially packaged), certain plant products
  • ✅ Freely allowed: Clothing, electronics (personal use), books, non-perishable food (packaged), gifts, jewellery

Step 4: Documents Required for Sending Parcel from India to USA

Having the correct paperwork ready prevents customs delays and ensures smooth clearance at US Customs.

Documents Checklist

  • ✅ Sender ID: Aadhar Card, Passport, or Driving Licence
  • ✅ Airway Bill (AWB): Generated by NRI Xpress upon booking
  • ✅ Commercial Invoice: Required for goods. Shows item description, quantity, and declared value in USD.
  • ✅ Packing List: Detailed list of all items with individual values
  • ✅ Customs Declaration Form (CN23): Required for all international parcels
  • ✅ For Medicines: Doctor's prescription copy + declaration letter
