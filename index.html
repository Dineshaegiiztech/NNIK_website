Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about sending parcels internationally from Coimbatore.

International courier charges from Coimbatore start from ₹550/kg to UAE, ₹850/kg to UK, ₹950/kg to USA and Canada, and ₹900/kg to Australia. Rates depend on the destination country, parcel type, weight, and dimensions. Use our free rate calculator for an instant estimate.

Express shipping from Coimbatore to USA takes 3 –5 Business Days. Economy/standard shipping can take 10–14 days. We partner with DHL, FedEx, and UPS for reliable delivery. Documents typically arrive within 3–4 business days via express.

Yes! NRI Xpress specializes in medicine delivery abroad. We handle all required documentation including prescriptions and customs declarations. Common medicines for personal use are generally allowed in most countries. Contact us for destination-specific regulations. Learn more about medicine shipping.

Yes! We offer door-to-door pickup from anywhere in Coimbatore via porter and surrounding areas including Tiruppur, Erode, and Salem. Simply WhatsApp or call us to schedule a pickup at your convenient time. Our team will collect the parcel and deliver it to the nearest hub for dispatch.

For personal parcels: a valid ID, sender/recipient address details, and a list of contents with values. For medicines: a doctor's prescription copy. For commercial goods: an invoice and packing list. Our team guides you through the entire documentation process at no extra charge.

Once your shipment is dispatched, you receive a tracking number via WhatsApp and email. You can track it directly on our courier partner's website (DHL/FedEx/UPS) or by contacting our support team. Real-time updates are sent at every milestone from Coimbatore to delivery.
