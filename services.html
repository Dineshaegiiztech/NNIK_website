Medicine Courier from India to Abroad

One of our most popular services — shipping essential medicines,Homeo products, and healthcare items from India to NRIs abroad. We understand the urgency and sensitivity involved in medicine courier from India to USA, UK, and other countries.

Our team handles all necessary documentation including prescription copies, customs forms (FORM 3 for medicines), and country-specific import declarations to ensure smooth clearance.

  • Prescription medicines (with valid doctor's Rx)
  • Homeopathic medicines
  • Baby health products
  • Vitamins & supplements
  • Temperature-sensitive packaging available
  • Compliance with destination country import rules
⚠️ Important: Controlled substances (Schedule X) and narcotics cannot be shipped. Please consult us for specific medicine eligibility before shipping.
