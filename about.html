Our Story

From Coimbatore to the World

NRI Xpress was founded in 2008 by Mr. Krishnamurthy Rajan, a Coimbatore native who saw firsthand how difficult it was for families to send essentials to their loved ones abroad. What started as a small operation helping neighbors ship medicines and homemade food to their children in the USA and UK has grown into Coimbatore's most trusted international courier company.

Today, NRI Xpress serves over 15,000 customers and ships to 100+ countries across 6 continents. Our team of 45 dedicated professionals handles everything from urgent document delivery to monthly grocery boxes for NRI families.

We are proud partners of DHL Express — ensuring your parcels are handled by the world's best logistics networks.
